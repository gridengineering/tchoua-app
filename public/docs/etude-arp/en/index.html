01 · Orientations for product leadership

Summary and structural decisions

According to the manual provided, Tchoua has the essential objects of an association management package: association, member, activity, session and movement. Moving to ARP means making the rules explicit, the commitments calculable and the evidence accessible, then extending coverage to projects, resources, purchases and community services.

DecisionConsequence for the product
Common management coreSeparate user account, membership, contract, debt, payment and allocation. Each module consumes the same references.
Separate funds and accountsWhere funds are held does not define their permitted use. Budget, reservation and liquidity become three separate measures.
Versioned rulesStore base, rate, unit, effective date, exceptions and approval decision. Replay historical calculations.
Non-deactivatable invariantsIsolation of associations, uniqueness of payments, audit, preservation of amounts and concurrency control.
Progressive extensionStabilise the financial core before automating auctions, integrated payments and consolidation.
Risk-based test recipeCover normal cases, limits, errors, concurrency, recovery after failure and interactions between modules.

What must be decided before development

The product committee validates the priority models, the launch countries, the decision authorities, the treatment of debts on exit, the rounding policy and the funds authorised for each product. The competent experts validate the legal, accounting and payment frameworks required to activate the products concerned.

Scope of completeness. A finished study cannot enumerate every future status or every possible amount. The completeness sought is structural: a catalogue of dimensions, constraints, combination models and test criteria. New exceptions are added as versioned rules, not as hidden processing.

D01
Domain 01 · Operational sheet

Identity and forms of organization

The framework distinguishes the form of organization, the declared legal status and the operating profile. A friendly society can run a tontine without becoming a tontine in the sense of its identity. A federation can bring together autonomous entities or internal sections; this difference governs ownership of funds, authorizations and consolidation. Configuration must support the family, the neighbourhood, the village, the professional network, the cultural or religious grouping, the sports club, the diaspora, the mutual society and the cooperative. These categories are functional models and do not automatically confer a legal qualification.

D03
Domain 03 · Operational sheet

Memberships, members and exits

Membership is a contract between a person and an association, distinct from the login account. A member without a smartphone can be registered and assisted by an authorized agent; that agent signs for their own actions. The file follows application, review, acceptance, activation, suspension and exit. Voting, aid, credit and tontine rights depend on separate conditions. A removal does not settle a debt, and a death does not automatically transfer funds to the first person who comes forward. Legal persons have representatives whose mandates are dated.

Domain 04 · Operational sheet

Families, beneficiaries and confidentiality

Family aid requires a reference of related persons, events and evidence. The family link must not be a simple text copied into each request: its validity period, its verification status and the rights concerned must be known. The SLD rules on declared spouse, first marriage or birth are local examples from the document, not universal constraints of the package. The target makes them configurable and subject to validation. Health data is isolated from the information needed for accounting and transparency reports.

Multiple shares and shortened duration

Four members with two shares and four with one share represent twelve shares. Two entitlements distributed per session exhaust the entitlements in six sessions, but that is not enough to prove the funding. With an illustrative price of 50 000 per share, each session collects 600 000. Two entitlements of 300 000 are fundable; two entitlements of 550 000 would create a deficit of 500 000 per session without an additional contribution.

Opening invariant. Total planned contributions + identified funding ≥ promised distributions + planned charges + mandatory reserve. The system presents this equality to all subscribers before acceptance.

Fraction case: with b = 300 000, a minimum unit f = 0,25 and a pot of 250 000, three quarters of an entitlement cost 225 000, leaving 25 000. This distribution is permitted only if the contract allows beneficiary fractions and if the remaining entitlements allow them.
